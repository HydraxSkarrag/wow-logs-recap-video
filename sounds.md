# Sounds and music

Good sound makes a guild video. A ready check before a chapter, a boss voice when the boss shows up, the achievement sound when it finally dies.

## Game sounds

- **Wowhead sound database:** search for a boss, spell or UI sound and listen to it in the browser. Your agent can help you find the right file names (ready check, raid warning, achievement, level up, boss voices).
- World of Warcraft sounds belong to Blizzard. Check Blizzard's rules for fan content before you publish, and keep it to short sounds, not whole soundtracks.

## Music

- **YouTube Audio Library** (YouTube Studio > Audio Library): filter by genre, mood and "Attribution not required". Safe for YouTube, also for monetized videos.
- **Pixabay Music:** free to use. Some tracks are registered with Content ID and can trigger a claim on YouTube. Check the note on the track page.
- Search words that work for WoW: `fantasy ambient`, `medieval fantasy background`, `celtic fantasy`, `adventure orchestral underscore`, `cozy fantasy`, `no vocals`.
- For a video you watch while reading, pick something calm, without vocals, and at least as long as your video (or one that loops cleanly).

## Funny extras

- **Freesound** has effects like a sad trombone. Many sounds there are CC BY, which means you must credit the author in your description.

## Before you publish

- Write down source and license of every sound and song (`credits.md`).
- Put the credits into your video description.
- Turn the music down under clips that have their own sound.
