# Questions for your logs

Copy what you like, change the boss names, and ask your agent. Every answer in the video came from a question like these.

Tip: ask for the result **and** the evidence ("show me the report and fight for each number"). Then you can check it on Warcraft Logs.

## Progress and wipes

- Which bosses cost us the most wipes?
- Show our progress on <Boss>, pull by pull: boss health at every wipe, one bar per pull.
- What were our closest wipes? Boss health below 1 %.
- Which boss did we never kill, and how many pulls did we try?
- What was our longest fight? Kill or wipe?
- How many raid nights did each first kill take?
- Recap one raid: when did each boss fall, normal and heroic?
- Which night had the most wipes in a row?

## General stats

- How much time did we spend raiding? How much of it in boss fights?
- How many raid nights, pulls, kills, wipes and deaths in total?
- Show our final raid roster with class colors.
- Who was in at least 20 % of the raid weeks? That's our core group.
- Who interrupted the most? Who dispelled the most?
- Who was there for the most pulls?
- Which weekday did we raid most often?

## Awards

- Who drank the most potions? Also per 100 pulls.
- Who used the most healthstones?
- Who died first, most often? Leave out the tanks.
- Who was the last one standing when we wiped?
- Who saved us with soulstones and battle rezzes?
- Who did the most damage, and the most healing, over all expansions?
- Who hit their friends the most while mind controlled?

## Deaths

- Who died the most, over all expansions? And per 100 pulls?
- Who survived the most, per 100 pulls?
- What killed us the most? Top 3 abilities.
- Which boss mechanic hurt the most?
- How often did we fall to our death?
- Who died to the same mechanic again and again?

## Fun facts

- Did our tank wear the right gear? (Example: the Onyxia Scale Cloak against Shadow Flame in Blackwing Lair.)
- Who died at Nefarian because they forgot the cloak?
- Did anyone hold a fishing pole at a boss pull?
- Who tanked a boss without being a tank?
- What was our latest kill at night?
- Which report titles are the funniest?
- Who stood in the fire the most?
- Who pulled the boss too early?

## From numbers to a video

- Turn these results into scenes for a guild video. Show me a preview first.
- Make it look like World of Warcraft: dark panels, gold borders, class colors.
- Rebuild our most memorable fight with the real health bars from the log.
- Add sounds: boss voices, heals, deaths, and royalty-free music. Check every license.
- Make the music quieter while a clip with its own sound plays.
- Show me three stills of the video so I can check the layout.
- Looks good. Render the final video.
- Cut a 30-second teaser for the premiere.

## More video ideas

- Kill and wipe montage: every pull of one boss, back to back.
- Award show: your own awards, with a stage and a winner for every category.
- A teaser of 15 to 30 seconds to hype the premiere.
